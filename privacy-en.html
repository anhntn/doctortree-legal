English summary (short)

Pilot version pilot-2026-11-21. Effective 5 December 2026.

  • Who we are: the Doctor Tree development team; full legal-entity details will be added before general release. Data-protection contact: doctortree.x@gmail.com.
  • What we collect: phone number and password (hashed), name, province, language; optional avatar, birth date, email, farm area; gardens and crops; plant photos (max 3 per check, compressed) and text descriptions; diagnosis results, follow-ups, care tasks, garden diary; expert questions; store purchase IDs; your consent choices with policy version and server time; the dates you open the app (date only – no time, no location). For expert accounts: display name, province and district (entered by the expert). When you use voice diary / voice form-fill: the recognised text (sent to AI after filtering, not stored). We do not collect GPS location, ID numbers, bank/card numbers, voice recordings or health data. We never sell your data.
  • Consents: to diagnose you must tick two boxes: (1) storing photos to diagnose and (2) "Let AI learn from your photos" – plant photos and descriptions (without your name or phone number) are used to help the AI learn, to let verified experts check whether the AI was right, and to add anonymised cases to the shared knowledge base. New sign-ups without box (2) are refused; existing accounts without it are asked again at their next check. You can withdraw either box at any time in Settings → Privacy; while a box is unticked you cannot start new checks (re-check or extra photos included) until you tick it again – your account, gardens and diary are kept and you can still use the diary. Care contact (optional, Profile). Every grant/withdrawal is logged (append-only).
  • Where: Supabase servers in Singapore. Google Gemini API (paid tier; processed in the United States / globally – the Gemini API does not let us choose a region) receives only plant photos, descriptions and crop/garden info when diagnosing (no phone, no name), and the text of what you say in voice diary / voice form-fill after our server removes phone numbers, email addresses and ID-document numbers (names you say are still sent). From 1 November 2026 the same filter also applies to the description you write when diagnosing, what you tell us at a follow-up check, and the questions / answers used when suggesting shared-knowledge cases (phone numbers, emails and ID-card numbers removed; names and addresses are not filtered). In Vietnam (planned – applies once switched on): an SMS provider not yet chosen (processing in Vietnam) sends your phone-verification code (receives phone number + code); Zalo (VNG) – our official Zalo OA for support (you message us when you need help) and verification codes via Zalo (ZNS); Zalo is already used to notify experts of new questions (question ID only, no personal data). The app does not receive messages from you by SMS or Zalo. The first release of the app does not use the Zalo OA, Zalo (ZNS) codes or SMS codes (moved to a later release). For help, you add the Zalo account of our support staff yourself (number shown in the app: Settings → Help; 7:00–21:00 daily). The app sends nothing to Zalo; what you choose to send via Zalo is processed by Zalo (VNG Corporation, Vietnam) under Zalo's privacy policy, and our staff use it only to help you and do not keep it for other purposes. Backups: our server makes encrypted (AES-256-GCM) backups of the database (accounts and tables, no photos) and keeps them in a private store of the same Supabase project in Singapore – never on a personal computer. Anthropic PBC – Claude API (United States) may be used as a backup or replacement AI for Google Gemini when we change or add AI models; when used, it receives only the same kinds of data as Gemini above (plant photos, filtered descriptions and voice text, crop/garden info, anonymised shared-knowledge text) – no phone number, no name. Gemini (paid tier) and Anthropic API data are not used to train their models. A cross-border transfer impact dossier is filed under Article 20 of Law 91/2025/QH15.
  • Expert accounts: an expert's commune location and specialty crops are used to route questions to experts near the garden; they are not shown to farmers.
  • Who sees it: you; experts answering your question see photos/description/crop/province but not your phone, birth date or email; experts reviewing shared-knowledge cases (all verified experts plus up to 3 unverified experts in the same or a neighbouring province) see only the de-identified text (no name, phone number, garden name or photo) – only verified experts decide what enters the knowledge base; verified experts checking the weekly quality sample (up to 20 random cases a week, only from users who ticked box (2)) see the photos and the de-identified description to check the AI's answer; app admins when needed; other users see an expert's display name.
  • Shared knowledge base: only cases of users who currently tick box (2); reviewed by verified experts and checked for pesticide rules before entry; anonymised (no phone, email, names, garden name; province only); no photo copies. If you withdraw or delete your account, anonymised cases stay but are unlinked from you.
  • Voice: speech recognition uses the phone's own Apple/Google service, on the device where possible; if the phone cannot recognise speech on the device, the audio may go through Apple/Google servers (free, not through our servers). The app keeps only the text, never recordings. With voice diary / voice form-fill, the recognised text (never the audio) goes through our server to Google Gemini to prepare a draft for you, after phone numbers, emails and ID numbers are removed; the app does not store what you said.
  • Retention: until you delete; photos of cases closed for more than 24 months are deleted automatically; app-open dates are deleted after 180 days. Consent records are kept for 3 years after account deletion, without name, phone number or account ID (admin access only), then deleted automatically. Encrypted backups: 14 daily copies (14 days) now; after we move to the Supabase Pro plan (around January 2027), 8 weekly copies (8 weeks) plus Supabase's own daily backups kept for 7 days; older copies are deleted automatically. Payment records (amount, purchase date, package, transaction ID) are kept for 10 years from the end of the year of the transaction to meet our accounting and tax obligations (Vietnam Law on Accounting 2015, Decree 174/2016), then deleted automatically; they no longer contain your phone number or anything that identifies you and are not linked to any account in the app.
  • Your rights: access, correct, delete (account deletion in the app), withdraw consent, complain; data download in-app (Settings → Download my data).
  • Delete account: Settings → Delete account (photos included) – you tick all confirmation boxes (including "anonymised cases the AI has learned from are kept"); data and photos are erased immediately; anonymised shared cases, AI cost statistics (unlinked) and de-identified consent records (3 years) remain. Deleted data (not photos) may remain in the encrypted backups until those backups expire – at most 14 days now, at most 8 weeks after the move to the Pro plan – and is then deleted automatically. Payment records are kept anonymised for 10 years (see Retention).
  • Contact: doctortree.x@gmail.com; or message our support staff on Zalo – number shown in the app, 7:00–21:00 daily (Settings → Help).
