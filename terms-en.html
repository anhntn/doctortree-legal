Pilot version pilot-2026-11-21. Effective date: 5 December 2026. Read together with the Privacy Policy (pilot-2026-11-21).

Summary for farmers: the App gives suggestions from AI and experts – it does not replace an extension officer, plant-protection officer or agronomist visiting your orchard; the final decision is yours. For pesticides the App only names active ingredients permitted in Viet Nam, always with a pre-harvest interval, biological measures first – read the product label and ask your local extension officer before use. One account per phone number; keep your password private. Free checks are limited; buy more credits through the App Store / Google Play; purchased credits never expire; refunds follow Apple / Google policy and refunded credits are deducted. Your photos and text remain yours; when you tick "Let AI learn", anonymised cases may enter the shared knowledge base. You can delete your account at any time; if we lock an account for abuse you can still download your data and delete the account.

1. Scope and acceptance

  • Provider ("we"): the Doctor Tree development team (full legal-entity details will be added before general release) – email doctortree.x@gmail.com. Service: the Doctor Tree app for iPhone and Android and its server services (photo + AI diagnosis of fruit-tree problems, care plans, today's tasks, re-checks, garden diary, season calendar, ask-an-expert, credit purchases). Users ("you"): growers in Viet Nam; expert and admin accounts have extra rules in section 7. The Privacy Policy forms part of these Terms.
  • Creating an account or continuing to use the App means you accept these Terms; if you do not agree, do not use the App.
  • The App is for adult growers.

2. What the service is – and is not

  1. AI suggests, it does not diagnose in place of a professional. Results are generated by an AI model from your photos and description, with a confidence level. The AI can be wrong, especially with blurry, dark or distant photos or rare diseases. Results are not the conclusion of a plant-protection or extension officer who has visited your orchard.
  2. Some results wait for an expert. For crops or disease groups where the App is not yet confident enough, or when the AI reports "unclear", the App withholds the chemical part and sends the case to an expert for confirmation within the time in section 7; biological / cultural measures are shown first.
  3. Support level per crop. Some crops have deep support (own knowledge base and season calendar); others have general support (lower confidence, with a warning). Season calendars, today's tasks and care plans are regional, seasonal suggestions, not a substitute for observing your own orchard.
  4. The decision is yours. What to spray, what to feed, when to prune and when to harvest is your decision after checking product labels, your orchard's conditions and local officers' advice. We are not present in your orchard and cannot control how you act.
  5. No guarantee of crop outcome. We do not promise that trees will recover, that yield will rise or that produce will meet any standard (including GlobalGAP). The App helps you keep records in the spirit of GlobalGAP; certification is decided by the certification body.
  6. The App may pause. When the AI budget cap is reached, during maintenance or incidents, some functions (especially AI diagnosis) may pause or queue; gardens, diary and calendar keep working. We try to announce in the App when possible.

3. Pesticides – important disclaimer

  1. The App only names active ingredients listed in the List of pesticides permitted for use in Viet Nam (currently Circular 75/2025/TT-BNNMT as amended by Circular 28/2026/TT-BNNMT and successors), registered for the right crop and right pest/disease, and not banned. The App names no brands, sells no pesticides and takes no commission from sellers.
  2. Every chemical suggestion comes with a pre-harvest interval (PHI); where no label value is available the App marks the figure as an estimate. Biological and cultural measures always come first.
  3. You are responsible for: (a) reading and following the product label (label dose, target and PHI prevail over the App if they differ); (b) checking the product is permitted for your crop at the time of use (the list changes); (c) respecting the PHI for every crop in the plot, including intercrops harvested earlier; (d) asking local extension / plant-protection officers when unsure; (e) for export produce, asking the buyer about the importing country's maximum residue limits (MRL).
  4. Every suggestion carries the sentence: "This information is for reference only. Read the product label carefully and consult your local extension officer before use." It forms part of these Terms.
  5. To the extent permitted by law we are not liable for damage caused by use contrary to the label, wrong dose or crop, counterfeit or sub-standard products, failure to respect the PHI, residue exceedances, weather, or deviation from the suggestion.

4. Account, password, confirmation SMS

  1. Your account is tied to your phone number and a password (at least 6 characters). If you forget the password you send an SMS from that number to the App's short code to confirm; the SMS is charged by your carrier. Normal sign-in needs no SMS.
  2. Keep your password secret and do not share the account; actions from your account are treated as yours unless you have told us you lost control of it. One account per person; profile information must be truthful.
  3. Expert accounts are opened only with an expert code issued by us and approved by an admin; an expert's display name may not contain phone numbers or links.
  4. On sign-up the App asks you to tick the consent boxes described in Privacy Policy section 3 (storing photos for diagnosis; letting AI learn – the latter can be switched off server-side if the lawyer / Apple require). Until the required boxes are ticked, the account is not created.
  5. We may lock an account on signs of a breach of section 8, credit fraud, or at the request of a competent authority. The App shows the reason; you can still download your data and delete the account. You may respond through the channel in section 15.

5. Credits, packs, in-app purchases

5.1 What a credit is. One credit = one check you actively submit (up to 3 photos and/or a text or voice description) for which the App returns a valid result. Not charged: photos rejected before the AI runs (not a plant, too poor); technical errors on our side; re-opening old results; re-checks of a case being followed; reminders; diary entries (including by voice, with a daily limit). Charged: new checks; asking an expert about a case (charged like a check – even if the expert answers after the deadline in section 7; no refund). Asking an expert while the AI is paused or without a case: free, up to 3 questions per day.

5.2 Free plan and credit packs. Free – first 7 days: 3 credits/day, no roll-over. Free – from day 8: 5 credits/week, reset Monday 0:00 (Viet Nam time), no roll-over; when the AI budget cap is reached, free credits queue and run when the budget reopens. Nhà Nông pack: 50 credits; Chăm Vườn pack: 120 credits – one-off purchases that never expire.

  • The price is the price shown in the App Store (Apple) or Google Play (Google) at the time of purchase, taxes included per store rules; figures inside the App are only a fallback.
  • Pack credits are used before free credits. Credits belong to the account and are shared across all gardens. Garden limits: Free 2 · Nhà Nông 5 · Chăm Vườn 10; your tier = highest pack ever bought, never downgraded; over the limit you cannot add gardens but lose no data.
  • Credits are not convertible to cash, not transferable, and are lost when you delete your account (section 11).
  • We may change credits / prices for packs sold in future; packs you already bought keep their credits. Changes are announced in the App at least 7 days before they apply.

5.3 Purchase, restore, refund.

  1. Payment is processed by Apple / Google under their terms; we never receive your card or bank details. After the store confirms, our server verifies the receipt and adds credits once per transaction (re-sending a receipt adds nothing).
  2. If the store reports the transaction as pending (e.g. parental approval, delayed payment), credits are not added yet; tap Restore purchases once the store completes it.
  3. Restore purchases (new phone, reinstall): credits live on your server account – sign in and they are there; Restore is only needed when money was taken but credits did not arrive.
  4. Refunds follow the Apple / Google refund policy; request them from the store. When the store refunds or revokes a transaction we deduct the pack's credits (balance may go negative if already used; later purchases offset it); we do not lock the account or downgrade your tier.
  5. We sell no subscriptions (no auto-renewal, no monthly charges).

6. Your content and the shared knowledge base

  1. Photos, descriptions, diary entries and questions you submit ("your content") remain yours. You allow us to store, process and display them to provide the service to you (diagnosis, care plans, diary, expert questions) as described in the Privacy Policy.
  2. While you tick "Let AI learn from your photos", plant photos and descriptions (without your name or phone number) are also used to train the AI, to let experts check the AI, and to add anonymised cases to the shared knowledge base that helps other growers – exactly as in Privacy Policy sections 3, 5 and 6. Anonymised cases are kept after you untick or delete your account (no longer linked to you); original photos are deleted with the account.
  3. You warrant that what you upload is your own plants / orchard or that you are allowed to photograph it; contains no faces, documents, licence plates or other people's personal data; is lawful, not abusive, not advertising. Non-plant photos are rejected (no credit charged); infringing content may be removed and the account locked.
  4. Expert answers, AI results, season calendars and the knowledge base belong to us or to the cited source; use them for your own orchard, do not bulk-copy, resell or use them to advertise pesticides under the App's name.

7. Experts

  1. Experts are crop / plant-protection professionals we invite and approve; the "Verified" badge means an admin checked their profile under our internal process – not a state certification.
  2. Experts answer within 24 working hours; questions outside working hours queue; if overdue the App tells you but no credit is refunded (5.1). An expert may ask for more photos first.
  3. An expert's answer is a professional opinion for reference based on photos and text, not a substitute for an on-site visit; experts must also follow section 3 (permitted active ingredients, PHI, no brands) and the server re-checks the pesticide part before it is shown.
  4. Experts see the photos, description, crop and province of the case you ask about; not your phone number, date of birth or email. Experts reviewing shared cases or the weekly AI quality sample see only de-identified text (Privacy Policy section 5).
  5. Experts must answer honestly, name no brands, sell nothing, not contact growers outside the App to sell products, and not copy grower data out of the App. Breaches lead to loss of the badge / account lock.
  6. Experts are not paid through the App in the current version; any future payment will be under a separate agreement.
